The gear I carry and the desk I come back to…

What it is

  • Two optional fields per post: a next step and a size, Quick or Deeper.
  • The manual queue order stays the plan. The filter only narrows it; dragging is off while filtered, so the order is never rewritten from a subset.
  • Stored in the CMS database beside the queue order. Nothing is written to the Markdown or published.

Left out on purpose

  • No scores, deadlines, statuses or required steps. A post with neither field looks as it does today.
  • No suggestions or topic research inside cfcms. Another tool can write the same fields through the triage form action.
