Home list on mobile

Two problems in the current screen: Publish sits in a footer away from the stages it acts on, and that footer lands under the iOS Safari floating bar (red dashed). Rows spend a second line on the date.
  • Every variant keeps the bottom 96px free of controls. Nothing is fixed to the bottom.
  • Rows are one title line (wraps to two at most). Date is a short muted stamp on the right of the first line, so it adds no height. Status is a 7px dot: hollow = draft, amber = ready, hollow amber = changed, green = live.
  • Grip and × removed on touch: long-press to reorder, swipe left to remove / delete (already wired for swipe).
